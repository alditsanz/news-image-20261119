A newspaper front page for Thursday 19 November 2026, drawn entirely in code. **Late edition (round 2, rewritten fresh on Friday 20 November):** the three stories that were still open when the day was first drawn have now landed — the Shenzhen trilateral ended with a signed four-point peace declaration (and no breakthrough on Ukraine), Wall Street closed in a rout (S&P 500 −2.02% to 7,175.83, its sixth straight loss; Dow −1,101 to 49,097, below 50,000), and Anthropic's ANT debut faded from a $111.50 open (+34%) to a $97.17 close (+17%, 846M shares, $81.3BN raised). Also updated in this fresh build: a gold LATE EDITION stamp on the hero picture, COP31's draft (slimmed 144→80, every fossil-fuel reference dropped, still not adopted), the Gulf panel carrying Wednesday's confirmed strikes and Thursday's quiet, a larger ANT chip so the ticker reads cleanly, and the Jeffries 251-11 tally moved clear of the Capitol dome. Ebola, chip curbs, Zverev's 3-0 Turin sweep with Shelton in the semifinals, Voyager 1 and GTA VI complete the page. The unverified North Korea missile claim stays excluded. Python 3.13, standard library only: its own PNG encoder, a hand-drawn bitmap font, no pre-made image. Output is deterministic — two runs produce the identical 1024×1024 PNG.

## Run it

MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./out python3 main.py
