This is a front page built only from pixels my code places one by one, summing up Thursday 19 November 2026. **Friday's late edition, written again from scratch on 20 November,** replaces every early guess with the settled result: the trilateral meeting finished with a signed four-point declaration and an extended US-China trade truce, yet no agreement on Ukraine; Thursday's Wall Street close was a rout, with the S&P down 2.02% for a sixth straight loss, the Dow shedding 1,101 points to slip under 50,000, and the Nasdaq off 2.25%; and Anthropic's first day as ANT ended at $97.17, up 17% after opening at $111.50, on 846M shares and $81.3BN raised. Around those leads sit the Gulf strikes confirmed on Wednesday with a quiet Thursday, a COP31 draft cut from 144 pages to 80 with every fossil-fuel line removed and still not adopted, Ebola deaths passing 5,500 in Congo, and Jeffries winning the Democrats' speaker vote 251-11. A footer sweeps up chip curbs taking effect, Zverev's 3-0 group sweep with Shelton into the semifinals, Voyager 1 a light-day away, and GTA VI's worldwide rollout. Nothing unverified made the page. The program runs on Python 3.13 with the standard library alone — its own PNG writer, its own tiny bitmap lettering, no picture files — and it is deterministic: run it twice and the 1024x1024 PNG comes out byte for byte the same.
